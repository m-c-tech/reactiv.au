About

Built for the dancefloor,
engineered from scratch

reactiv.earrings started as a personal project and turned into something you can actually wear. Every animation, every palette, every pixel — custom built.

The story

reactiv.earrings grew out of a personal challenge to build an LED matrix small enough to hang from an ear before a local arts festival. The electronics, enclosures, the firmware, every animation, all designed and written from scratch.

Most wearable LED jewellery have bulky batteries, are conspicuous, and rarely something you'd wear anywhere other than a festival. We wanted to find a way to bring reactive colour and movement to somewhere it had never really been before: your ears.

FAQ

Questions?

How long does the battery last?

3 to 4 hours per charge, depending on animation mode. Brighter, more active modes like Audio Visualizer will draw slightly more power.

How do I charge them?

The earrings charge via the included carry case, which has a USB-C port. Just place the earrings in the case, plug in a USB-C cable, and they'll charge up ready for your next event.

Are they water resistant?

Not really — the earrings are rated IP52: protected against dust ingress and light dripping water. They will handle sweat and light splashes without issue. We recommend avoiding submerging them or wearing them in heavy rain.

How do I switch animation modes?

The button on each earring controls everything:

  • On / Off — Single click to put the earring to sleep; single click again to wake it.
  • Change animation — Double click to advance to the next animation mode.
  • Change colour — Long press (~1.2 s) to enter colour select mode. Once in that mode, single click steps through the available colour palettes. When you land on the one you want, another long press confirms your choice and returns to normal operation.
Do they react to music automatically?

Yes. The Audio Visualizer mode uses the onboard microphone to pick up ambient sound in real time — no pairing or app required. Just switch to the mode and the earrings respond to whatever is playing around you.
